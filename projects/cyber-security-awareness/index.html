Common Cybersecurity Threats

Malware

Malicious software designed to harm your computer or steal data

Prevention:
  • Use antivirus software
  • Keep software updated
  • Avoid suspicious downloads

Social Engineering

Manipulating people to reveal confidential information

Prevention:
  • Verify identity before sharing info
  • Be skeptical of urgent requests
  • Never give passwords over phone

Unsecured Wi-Fi

Public networks that can expose your data to attackers

Prevention:
  • Use VPN on public Wi-Fi
  • Avoid accessing sensitive accounts
  • Turn off auto-connect

Ransomware

Malware that encrypts files and demands payment

Prevention:
  • Regular backups
  • Email security training
  • Network segmentation

Phishing Awareness

Phishing attacks trick you into revealing personal information through fake emails, websites, or messages.

Spot the Phishing Email

From: security-team@paypaI.com (Notice the 'I' instead of 'l')
Subject: URGENT: Your account will be suspended!

Dear Customer,

We detected suspicious activity on your account. Click here immediately to verify your identity or your account will be suspended within 24 hours!

PayPal Security Team

🚨 Red Flags:

  • Urgent language and threats
  • Misspelled domain (paypaI vs paypal)
  • Generic greeting
  • Suspicious call-to-action button
  • Poor grammar and formatting

How to Protect Yourself

Verify the Sender

Check email addresses carefully for misspellings or suspicious domains

Hover Before Clicking

Hover over links to see where they actually lead before clicking

Contact Directly

If unsure, contact the company directly using official contact information

Use Browser Security

Keep your browser updated and use security extensions

Safe Browsing Practices

Check for HTTPS

Always look for the padlock icon and "https://" before entering sensitive information

https://secure-site.com ✓ Secure
http://unsecure-site.com ⚠ Not Secure

Download Safety

Only download software from official sources and scan files before opening

Verify publisher/developer
Read user reviews
Scan with antivirus

Avoid Malicious Ads

Use ad blockers and be cautious of too-good-to-be-true offers
